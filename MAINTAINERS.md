## Affiliation disclosure

Both initial maintainers work at Automattic, the company that builds WordPress.com and contributes substantially to WordPress core. The plugin was incubated there before being published in the open. The repo lives under the `WordPress` GitHub org (or, during the staging phase, `Automattic`) — community-branded, not Automattic-product-branded — so anyone in the WordPress community can contribute on equal footing.

Decisions are made on the merits in public threads (Issues, Discussions, PR review). No private decision-making channel takes precedence over public discussion. Where a decision needs a tie-breaker, the engineering lead has the final call on engineering matters and the design lead on design matters; in both cases the rationale is documented in the relevant Issue or Discussion.

## How to reach us

- **Public**: GitHub Issues / Discussions on this repo.
- **Make/Core**: discussions tagged `feature: navigation`.

## How to become a maintainer

We grow the maintainer set by demonstrated, sustained contribution. The bar is roughly:

- Contributed at least 5 merged PRs that have aged well (no regressions, reviewers thank you).
- Actively engaged in Discussion + Issue triage.
- Worked on at least one non-trivial feature or fix (not just docs / typo PRs — though those are very welcome too).

We add maintainers by consensus of the existing set. If you want to grow into the role, the path is to keep contributing and engage in the public triage / review work.

## Project governance for non-maintainer decisions

For decisions that affect the project's posture but not its day-to-day code:

- **API stability promises**: discussed in a Make/Core post + Discussion thread. Lock at v1.0.
- **Submission to wp.org plugin directory**: maintainer decision after community signal stabilizes (target v0.3.x).
- **Trademark / branding / slug changes**: requires WordPress Foundation alignment if it touches WP-trademarked terms.
- **Repo transfer / org change**: requires the destination org's Owner action; documented openly in the Issue thread that proposes the transfer.
